Welcome to DukaaOn

Empowering Retailers with Seamless Logistics, Supply Chain, and Financial Solutions.

About Us

DukaaOn is a technology-driven platform providing small and medium-sized retailers with financial empowerment and efficient logistics solutions.

Who We Serve

We cater to rural and semi-urban retailers, helping them streamline inventory and access affordable credit.

What We Offer

Easy loans, stock sharing, micro-warehousing, and direct connections with wholesalers and manufacturers.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower retailers by leveraging technology to simplify supply chains, inventory management, and financial accessibility.

Our Vision

Our vision is to be the go-to platform for small retailers in Rural, Semi Urban India, bridging the gap between technology, logistics, and financial inclusion.

How We Operate

Using AI-driven tools, we optimize warehousing, connect retailers with wholesalers, and facilitate stock sharing.

Efficient Ordering

Retailers can place orders easily, and we ensure delivery using local micro-warehouses.

Stock Sharing

Nearby retailers with surplus stock fulfill urgent orders, ensuring no business disruptions.
